Privacy Policy

App: ACL · Operated by AL-AMANA COMMUNICATION LIMITED · Last updated: 26 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AL-AMANA COMMUNICATION LIMITED ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the ACL mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account information you provide or that is provisioned for you, such as your name, email address, phone number, and residence/lease details.
  • Content you submit in the App, including support tickets, messages, guest registrations, and maintenance requests.
  • Device push token used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Technical data needed for the App to function, such as authentication tokens stored securely on your device.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App's features (payments, invoices, ledger, tickets, messages, notices, guests, and account management).
  • To send you notifications and important account or building updates.
  • To respond to your requests and provide customer support.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the App (for example, push-notification delivery via Google Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service) and where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and comply with our legal obligations.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data directly in the App under Profile → Delete Account. Once deleted, your data is removed from our active systems, subject to any retention required by law.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections, and authentication credentials are stored using your device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
